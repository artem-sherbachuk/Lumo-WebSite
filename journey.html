The Journey

One road. Thirty chapters.
A whole language.

Lumo's map isn't a menu of lessons — it's a single winding road from a small harbor village up to a tower in the mountains. Walk it, and the language walks with you.

How the map works

The map is the curriculum

Each region of the world is a CEFR level. Each stop on the road is a chapter — a real place with real people, a small quest, and the exact language you need to get through it.

  • A1 — The Harbor Village (Ch. 1–5). First words by the sea: the café, meeting Luka, the road to the lighthouse, the morning market, the guesthouse.
  • A2 — The Coast Road (Ch. 6–10). The bus to Budva, a day at the beach, a lost bag, the old master's garden, the turn to the lake.
  • B1–B2 — Lakes & Highlands (Ch. 11–20). Longer stories, faster conversations, real opinions — the language starts to feel like yours.
  • C1–C2 — The Mountain Road (Ch. 21–30). Nuance, humor, idioms. The tower at the top is waiting for those who finish the story.

Inside a chapter

What one stop on the road holds

A living scene

A place on the coast rendered like a storybook — with characters who greet you, tasks to complete, and secrets to find by tapping around.

Phrases to speak & write

Each chapter tracks what you've spoken aloud and what you've written. Master both to truly complete the chapter — not just tap through it.

A unique reward

Finish everything and collect the chapter's keepsake — each one hand-crafted for its place in the story. Your shelf of rewards is your progress bar.
