Croatian Basics:
Your First 20 Phrases

These are the phrases that open doors — literally. Learn them and you can walk into any café, market, or guesthouse on the coast and be met with a smile.

Croatian and Serbian speakers are famously warm toward anyone who tries their language. You don't need perfect grammar — you need a handful of honest phrases and the courage to say them. Here they are, grouped the way you'll actually use them. Where Serbian differs, we show it after the slash.

Greetings & politeness

Dobar dan.Good day. — The universal, always-safe greeting. Dobro jutro. / Dobro veče.Good morning. / Good evening (Serbian; Croatian: dobra večer). Bok! / Zdravo!Hi! — “Bok” is Croatian and casual; “zdravo” works everywhere. Hvala!Thank you! — The word Lumo's own app icon shouts with joy. Molim.Please — and also “you're welcome,” and also “pardon?” A tiny word that does everything. Izvinite. / Oprostite.Excuse me / I'm sorry. (Serbian / Croatian preference.) Doviđenja!Goodbye! — Formal but friendly; “ćao” works with people you know.

At the café

Café culture is sacred on the Adriatic. A coffee is never rushed — and neither is this vocabulary.

Molim jednu kafu.One coffee, please. (Croatian: kavu.) — Lumo's very first phrase. Jednu vodu, molim.One water, please. Račun, molim.The bill, please. Koliko košta?How much does it cost?

Getting around

Gde je...? / Gdje je...?Where is...? (Serbian / Croatian.) Levo. / Desno. / Pravo.Left. / Right. / Straight ahead. (Croatian: lijevo, ravno.) Kada polazi autobus?When does the bus leave? — You'll need this for the bus to Budva.

Making friends

Kako se zoveš?What's your name? Zovem se...My name is... Drago mi je!Nice to meet you! Ne razumem. / Ne razumijem.I don't understand. (Serbian / Croatian.) — Say it with a smile; help always follows. Govorite li engleski?Do you speak English? — Your safety rope, though you'll need it less each day. Sve je u redu.Everything's fine. — The unofficial motto of the whole coast.

How to actually remember these

Don't drill the list. Attach each phrase to a moment: imagine the waiter's face when you say “Molim jednu kafu”, the market stall where you ask “Koliko košta?”. Context is glue — that's the entire philosophy behind how Lumo teaches: every phrase belongs to a scene, a character, and a small victory.

Learn ten phrases in a story, and you'll remember them for years. Learn a hundred in a list, and they're gone by Tuesday.

Ready to say them out loud and get feedback? The first chapters of the Lumo journey teach exactly these phrases — in the café where they belong.
